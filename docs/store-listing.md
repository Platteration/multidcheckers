## Title

5D Checkers

## Short description

Checkers with time travel, branching boards and paradox puzzles.

## Description

Move checkers, jump pieces and crown kings across a growing map of timelines. Send a checker into a past board to create a branch, then answer every waiting board before the turn passes.

Play against three bot levels, pass one device between players, or exchange game codes by message. Learn through puzzles and watch move-by-move replays. Choose five board skins and four piece sets in light or dark mode. Cosmetic extras are available free in this build; billing is disabled.
