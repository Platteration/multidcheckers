## Title

5D Checkers

## Short description

Checkers with time travel, branching boards and paradox puzzles.

## Description

Move checkers, jump pieces and crown kings across a growing map of timelines. Send a checker into a past board to create a branch, then answer every waiting board before the turn passes.

Play against three bot levels, pass one device between players, or exchange game codes by message. Learn through puzzles and watch move-by-move replays. Choose five board skins and four piece sets in light or dark mode. Cosmetic extras are available free in this build; billing is disabled.

## Data notes for the privacy-policy author

Game progress and settings are stored locally. Game/challenge codes deliberately include information needed to replay a shared game; players choose who receives them. The phone apps make no network request. The website, once its sign-in and shop API is configured (README, "Sign-in and the shop"), is told by Google a visitor's account id and email address when they sign in, and Stripe handles the payment when they buy and is told the account id with it (never the email address); the site keeps the account id, what was bought and Stripe's customer id, and the email address only while they stay signed in, until they delete the account, which leaves the payment with Stripe under that account id. These notes do not replace a published privacy policy or a review of platform, hosting and future billing data practices.
